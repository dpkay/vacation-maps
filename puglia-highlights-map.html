Reading the spread

  • Everything lives in a ~30 km circle around the masseria triangle — white towns inland, lidos on the coast, caves and safari in between. No day exceeds ~40 min from either base.
  • Bari Vecchia is best on arrival-adjacent or departure logic (30–50 min north), Torre Guaceto + Ostuni pair naturally to the south.
  • Matera (1 h inland) is not a day trip — it's the built-in stop on the Sep 8 drive to Naples.

Coordinates approximate — for orientation, not navigation. ZTL warning: park outside every old town's walls.
