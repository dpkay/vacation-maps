Valais Aug 16–24: the highlight superset + Airbnb search zones

🏠 Where to search for the Airbnb (elevation = A/C; aim ≥ 1,200 m)

  • Grächen (1,620 m) — coolest; the Spieleweg is in-village; dead-end road, 30 min to Visp
  • Bürchen / Unterbäch (~1,350 m) — sunny terrace above the valley, Moosalp out the back door, 20 min to Visp
  • Visperterminen (1,340 m) — quiet wine-village terrace, 20 min to Visp, closest to Saas valley
  • Naters / Blatten-bei-Naters (700 / 1,320 m) — Brig side, closest to Aletsch + Brigerbad
  • Visp or Brig town (650–680 m) — fallback only: most supply, most central, but hot in August

The highlights (superset — more than the 8 days can hold)

  • Leisee / Sunnegga — swimmable lake + Wolli playground + Matterhorn (Täsch 25 min + train + funicular)
  • Riffelsee — Gornergrat railway, Rotenboden→Riffelberg walk (~2.5 km downhill)
  • Grächen: Ravensburger Spieleweg + Hannigalp — board-game trail, ~4 km (30 min)
  • Saas-Fee / Spielboden marmots — car-free village, hand-fed marmots (35 min)
  • Bettmeralp — car-free plateau, Blausee loop, Bettmerhorn glacier lookout (Betten lift, 35 min)
  • Riederalp — the quieter Aletsch plateau variant (Mörel lift, 30 min)
  • Fafleralp, Lötschental — flat glacier-river walk, Grundsee/Schwarzsee (40 min)
  • Gemmi / Daubensee — cable car above Leukerbad, flat lake loop (35 min + lift)
  • Moosalp — ponds, cows, easy trails, above Bürchen (25 min)
  • Leukerbad thermal — big pool complex, rainy-day banker (35 min)
  • Brigerbad thermal water park — outdoor slides, the low-effort day (15 min)
  • Bonus: Oeschinensee — arguably Switzerland's best kid mountain lake; reachable as a long day via the Lötschberg car train (~1h10) or saved for a future Bernese Oberland trip

Coordinates are approximate (village/lift-base level) — for orientation and Airbnb zoning, not navigation.
