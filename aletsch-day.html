Wednesday Aug 19 — Aletsch day (Bettmeralp)

Forecast: 20 °C at 1,950 m · 13.1 h sun · 3% rain — the plateau's best day of the week. Lines schematic — follow yellow signs. ~CHF 100–115 all-in (4× Halbtax, kids free).
Lift Walk (Option A) Walk (Option B traverse) Lake Station / village

Option A — standard (whole group)

  1. 08:00 depart Saas-Grund (~55 min) → Betten Talstation garage
  2. 09:15 cable car → Bettmeralp (village lifeline: runs ~06:00–23:00, no stranding risk)
  3. 09:45 Bettmerhorn gondola → glacier view deck in morning clarity (last descent ~16:30 — hence first)
  4. ~11:15 walk DOWN from the deck via the signed Blausee route (lake sits ~250 m above the village — descending to it from Bettmerhorn beats climbing to it; confirm gradient on the top-station panorama board). Picnic at the lake, continue down to the village. Carrier for Elio; stroller waits at the village station; grandparents ride the gondola down.
  5. 13:30–16:00 Bettmersee: pedalo, swim for the brave, big playground; grandparents on the lawn
  6. ~16:30 cable car down, home ~17:45

Option B — the traverse (THE DEFAULT — lake afternoons are what we've had for 5 weeks; the ridge is the scarce good. Take A only if legs/wind/mood argue at the deck)

  1. Bettmerhorn → Gletscherweg ridge to Moosfluh (~2.5 km, glacier alongside; carrier for Elio)
  2. Moosfluh gondola down → Riederalp: lunch, Villa Cassel garden or Aletschwald slice
  3. Flat Panoramaweg back to Bettmeralp (~2 km); Bettmersee closer if time; lift down
  4. B skips Blausee by design — the ridge outbids it. Grandparents: lift-served parallel track, reunite at Riederalp lunch. Stroller parks at Bettmerhorn village station, reclaimed on return.
Morning gate: Bettmeralp webcam at breakfast — fog against the odds → flip to a Saastal day. Pack: picnic, swim gear, carrier, fleeces for the deck (2,647 m), sunscreen.
