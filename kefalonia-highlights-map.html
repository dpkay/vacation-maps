Reading the spread

  • South cluster (Svoronata–Lassi–Lourdata): sandy kid beaches + airport + Argostoli — daily life under Variant A.
  • East-center cluster (Sami/Agia Efimia): both caves, Karavomilos lake, Antisamos — one compact cave-and-swim zone.
  • North string (Myrtos → Assos → Fiskardo/Emblisi/Foki): the scenic old-Kefalonia day(s) — farthest from any southern base.
  • Lixouri peninsula (Xi, Petani): reached via the Argostoli car ferry — a self-contained day.
